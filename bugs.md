# Bugs

1. Even if the protocol isn't synced, some of the handshake packets (e.g: `SetTime`) can be sent before the Server rejects the Client, causing one of them to crash (Most likely Client).
2. All shadows slide forward as the day moves on, then snap back, about once per second. Not the 30 s `SetTime` resync. Confirmed by the creator: it stops with `--freeze-time` and does not happen at 0,0. So it is the texel snapping in `Renderer::shadowMap`: the snap grid rotates with the sun, so the snapped centre hops by a texel (about 0.5 units at render distance 500, 0.2 at the 200-unit shadow radius), more often the farther the player is from the origin. Plan: snap the centre on a coarse world grid instead (about 20 units on x and z, map radius `GameState::getShadowRadius()` plus one cell), and later rebuild the map only when the sun has moved a few degrees (caching).
