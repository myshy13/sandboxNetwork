# Bugs

1. Even if the protocol isn't synced, some of the handshake packets (e.g: `SetTime`) can be sent before the Server rejects the Client, causing one of them to crash (Most likely Client).
2. Lines run through the shadows in the afternoon (seen at world time 0.72, sun about 11 degrees up). Likely shadow acne: the bias in `glsl330/lighting.fs` is a constant `0.0005`, too small for a grazing sun. Slope-scaled bias is on the plan; the map is also squashed into a thin sliver at that angle.
3. All shadows slide forward as the day moves on, then snap back, about once per second. Not the 30 s `SetTime` resync. Confirmed by the creator: it stops with `--freeze-time` and does not happen at 0,0. So it is the texel snapping in `Renderer::shadowMap`: the snap grid rotates with the sun, so the snapped centre hops by a texel (about 0.5 units at render distance 500), more often the farther the player is from the origin. Plan: own shadow radius (~200, texel 0.2) and rebuild the map only when the sun has moved a few degrees (caching).
